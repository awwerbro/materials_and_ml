# April 20, 2026 Meeting

**Date:** 2026-04-20  
**Presenter:** Mariana Fazio, STFC Ernest Rutherford Fellow, UK  
**Topic:** Bayesian Statistics and Error Propagation in Experimental Data  
**Paper/Resources:** 
- [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.17792](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.17792)
- [Kinisi package](https://github.com/kinisi-dev/kinisi)

**Summary:** Mariana presented a paper on Bayesian Methods for the Investigation of Temperature-Dependence in Conductivity, highlighting subtle but important considerations when analyzing experimental data. The discussion covered key concepts including Bayes theorem, heteroscedasticity (asymmetric error distributions), and the non-normal error propagation that occurs when linearizing the Arrhenius equation. 

The presentation sparked discussion about the relevance of these statistical considerations for experimental materials science (ellipsometry, XPS fitting), where measurement uncertainties may be dominated by systematic errors rather than the theoretical error distributions discussed in the paper. The group also noted the paper's use of the Kinisi package (even though this is used for molecular dynamics simulations and not one to one translatable to experimental science).

A particularly cool feature was the paper's "show your work" implementation, which embeds GitHub logos throughout the manuscript that link directly to the code for reproducing each figure and analysis.
